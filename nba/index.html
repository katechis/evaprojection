EVA Model Projections NBA

What gets posted

Full-game totals and spreads where the model's price is at least 3% better than the market line at posting. Each pick is graded at the line and price it was posted at, 1 unit per pick.

When

Picks post in the evening, shortly after the market opens the next day's lines. That is when the model's edge is largest; it fades over the following hours.

What the model uses

Player ratings from results and from where lines have closed, expected minutes, the official injury report, rest and travel, and recent pace and scoring form.

Record

Totals and spreads are tracked separately. Early-season picks (October) are shown but not counted.
