NFL Picks

How it works

EVA projects every NFL game from play-by-play data, adjusted for opponents, quarterbacks and injuries, and tested against years of betting markets. Every game gets a spread pick, graded at the line it was posted at. Every result counts.

Power Rankings

Rating is how many points better than an average NFL team each team would be on a neutral field, from the same model that sets the picks. Offense and defense are points scored and allowed per game compared with an average team, from team stats only, so they do not add up exactly to the rating. Rankings update Monday mornings.
